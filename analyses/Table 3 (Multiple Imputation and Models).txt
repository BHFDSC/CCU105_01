*Data preparation

*generate splines for age 
rcsgen mi_index_age, df(3) gen(agercs) orthog
global ageknots `r(knots)'
matrix R_age=r(R)

*Generate interaction terms
egen covid_sex_interaction = group (covid_period sex)
egen covid_mitype_interaction = group (covid_period discharge_diagnosis)

*Generate Nelson-Aalen estimatea (time to CKD & time to death)
stset survtime, failure(death_flag==1) scale(365.25)
sts gen na_mortality =  na

stset time_to_ckd, failure(ckd==1) scale(365.25)
sts gen na_ckd = na

*Create a numeric person id
gen pid = _n

*Use msset to declare and restructure the data for multi-state modelling
msset, id(pid) states(flag_ckd death_flag) times(time_to_ckd survtime)

*Store msset default transition matrix
mat tmat = r(transmatrix)
mat list tmat

*Fit a model by transition
stset _stop, enter(_start) failure(_status) scale(365.25)
